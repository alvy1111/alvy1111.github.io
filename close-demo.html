What it missed

Across the test companies it missed 3 of 96 planted errors. On their first runs, the two never-tuned companies got 1 and 2 wrong fixes. The sealed company got 0.

I haven't measured hours saved, so I'm not claiming any. I picked the error types by hand, and no outside auditor has tested it.

The controls

I also ran attacks against the controls: approvals, roles, segregation of duties and the audit log. In the latest run, 57 of 57 were refused.
